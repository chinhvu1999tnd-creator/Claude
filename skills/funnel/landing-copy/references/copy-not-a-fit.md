# Copy Specification: Not-a-Fit / Redirect Page

Reference file for the `landing-copy` skill. Use when producing a Copy Brief for a not-a-fit page — the page shown when someone submits an application but is not the right fit to book a call or enter a program at this time.

---

## Why This Page Matters

Most funnels skip this page entirely or redirect to the homepage. That is a major mistake.

**People who arrive at a not-a-fit page are one of three types:**

1. **Wrong timing** — in 6 months they could be your best customer
2. **Wrong offer** — they are a fit for a different offer of yours, not this one
3. **Genuinely not a fit** — but they will tell others about the experience

**Done right:** All three types leave feeling respected, helped, and not dismissed. Types 1 and 2 will come back. Type 3 will refer.

**Done wrong:** They feel dismissed, judged, or tricked into a form only to be told no. They will tell others.

**This is a brand touchpoint, not a dead end.**

---

## Master Stack

| Framework | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **Halbert Honest Specificity** | Explain clearly and truthfully — no vagueness, no hedging |
| **Kern Genuine Alternative** | Give them something genuinely useful — not a consolation prize |
| **Brunson New Opportunity** | Reframe: this is not rejection — it is a redirect to a better path |

---

## The Paradox of Exclusion Copy

Good exclusion copy does not only redirect the wrong people — it increases the desire of the right ones.

When a right-fit person reads a not-a-fit page and sees that they are NOT in the "not yet right" group, they feel special. They want in more.

Even this page must be written clearly enough that a right-fit person reading it knows they do not belong here.

---

## Output Format: Copy Brief

This page should output a **Copy Brief with 2 sections** in this order:

---

### Section 1: Narrative

**Type:** `narrative`
**Intent:** Reader understands why this is not the right fit right now — and feels respected, not rejected. The tone is that of a real advisor who cares about their outcome, not a system that turned them down.
**Typical EMPHASIS keywords:** The specific prerequisite they're missing, the reframe ("not yet" not "not ever"), the specific milestone to aim for
**Visual:** none

**Copy requirements:**

**Headline — the opening of the narrative:**

Do NOT use:
- "Rất tiếc bạn không đủ điều kiện" — blame-y and cold
- "Chúng tôi không thể tiếp nhận bạn lúc này" — corporate, distant
- "Xin lỗi, bạn không phù hợp" — apologetic and deflating
- "Cảm ơn bạn đã apply nhưng..." — soft rejection that feels worse than a clear one

DO use:
- Advisor framing: `"Dựa trên những gì bạn chia sẻ, đây là điều tôi nghĩ phù hợp hơn với bạn lúc này"`
- Timing reframe: `"Bạn đang ở đúng chỗ — chỉ là timing chưa đúng. Đây là bước tiếp theo tôi gợi ý."`
- Direct + caring: `"Tôi đọc kỹ application của bạn, và muốn thành thật: [offer này] chưa phải lúc phù hợp nhất cho bạn."`

Rules for the headline:
- Must sound like a person speaking, not a system rejecting
- No "Rất tiếc" or "Xin lỗi" — you did nothing wrong and neither did they
- Use "bạn" — personal, not "application của bạn"

**Explanation paragraph (maximum 3 sentences):**

Core principle: never blame the reader. Never imply they are not good enough. Frame as: this offer requires a certain foundation, and you are helping them build that foundation first.

Template:
```
"Không phải vì bạn không thể — mà vì [main offer] cần [foundation/prerequisite] mà bạn đang trong quá trình xây dựng.

Điều đó không phải điểm yếu — đó là điểm xuất phát của hầu hết người sau này làm tốt nhất.

Thay vì bắt đầu ở chỗ chưa phù hợp, tôi muốn gợi ý một hướng sẽ giúp bạn đến đích nhanh hơn."
```

Sentence structure:
- Sentence 1: the reason — frame as a prerequisite, not a failure
- Sentence 2: normalize — "most of the best people started here too"
- Sentence 3: transition to the alternative — "I want to suggest..."

Do NOT use passive-aggressive language: "Tiếc là chúng tôi không thể..." — just say the thing.

**Example Copy Brief output for this section:**
```
**Type:** narrative
**Intent:** Reader feels respected, understands specifically why this isn't the right fit right now, and is curious about the alternative rather than discouraged.
**Emphasis:** không phải vì bạn không thể, điểm xuất phát, đến đích nhanh hơn
**Visual:** none

**Copy:**
Dựa trên những gì bạn chia sẻ, đây là điều tôi nghĩ phù hợp hơn với bạn lúc này.

Chương trình 1:1 được thiết kế cho người đã có doanh thu và muốn hệ thống hóa để scale — không phải bắt đầu từ bước đầu tiên. Không phải vì bạn không thể — mà vì chúng ta sẽ không có đủ thứ để làm việc cùng nhau một cách có ý nghĩa ngay lúc này. Tôi muốn gợi ý một hướng sẽ giúp bạn đến đây nhanh hơn.
```

---

### Section 2: CTA Strip

**Type:** `cta-strip`
**Intent:** Reader accepts the alternative offer or free resource as a genuinely helpful next step — and knows exactly when to come back for the main offer. The door stays open without pressure.
**Typical EMPHASIS keywords:** The alternative offer name, specific benefits relevant to their situation, the specific milestone to reach before returning
**Visual:** none

**Copy requirements:**

This section has two parts: (1) the alternative offer, and (2) keeping the door open.

**Part 1 — Alternative offer:**

Kern's logic: the alternative must be given because it genuinely helps — not to avoid losing the lead. If you are recommending an alternative just to keep someone in your funnel, they will feel it.

If you have a relevant alternative offer:
```
"Đây là điều tôi nghĩ phù hợp hơn với giai đoạn của bạn:

[Offer name / resource / program]

Nó sẽ giúp bạn:
→ [Benefit 1 — specific and relevant to their situation]
→ [Benefit 2]
→ [Benefit 3 — should connect to the prerequisite you named in Section 1]

[CTA Button: "[Active action]"]"
```

If no relevant alternative offer exists — use a free resource instead:
```
"Trong lúc này, đây là thứ tôi nghĩ sẽ giúp ích nhất cho bạn:

[Resource name — guide, video, checklist]

[1 sentence: what it helps them do specifically]

[CTA Button: "Nhận miễn phí" or "Xem ngay"]"
```

Rules for the alternative:
- Must be genuinely relevant to their specific situation — not generic
- Present as "the right path for where you are right now" — not "you're not enough for this, but here's something easier"
- No urgency language on this CTA — no pressure, no countdown

**Part 2 — Keep the door open:**

Leave a bridge for when they are ready. No urgency. No pressure.

Template:
```
"Khi bạn đã [specific, achievable milestone], tôi muốn nói chuyện.

[Zalo link / số] — cứ nhắn thẳng khi đến lúc đó. Tôi sẽ nhớ."
```

Rules for the milestone:
- Must be SPECIFIC — not "khi bạn sẵn sàng" (meaningless)
- Must be achievable in 3–12 months — not too far, not too near
- Zalo contact must be personal — not a page or bot
- No urgency language — "Chỗ còn hạn chế!" is the opposite of the tone needed here
- "Tôi sẽ nhớ" or equivalent — warm closing, not transactional

**Example Copy Brief output for this section:**
```
**Type:** cta-strip
**Intent:** Reader sees the alternative as a genuine path forward, takes the next step, and knows exactly when to come back for the main offer.
**Emphasis:** Khóa học Offer Lab, 10 khách hàng trả tiền, nhắn thẳng
**Visual:** none

**Copy:**
Đây là điều tôi nghĩ phù hợp hơn với giai đoạn của bạn:

Khóa học Offer Lab — Xây và Validate Offer Đầu Tiên Trong 4 Tuần

Nó sẽ giúp bạn:
→ Tìm ra niche và offer cụ thể mà người thật sẵn sàng trả tiền — trước khi đốt thời gian vào thứ chưa được test
→ Có 10 khách hàng đầu tiên bằng cách tiếp cận thủ công nhưng hiệu quả — không cần ads, không cần website
→ Xây nền tảng đúng để khi bạn muốn scale, bạn biết chính xác mình đang scale cái gì

[Tìm hiểu về Offer Lab]

---

Khi bạn có khách hàng trả tiền đầu tiên và muốn xây hệ thống để lặp lại, nhắn thẳng Zalo tôi: [số].

Đó là lúc chúng ta có thể làm việc cùng nhau tốt nhất. Tôi sẽ nhớ.
```

## Tone Guide

**You are:** A real advisor who cares about the other person's outcome — not a salesperson who just lost a deal.

| Salesperson who lost a deal | Advisor who genuinely cares |
|-----------------------------|------------------------------|
| "Rất tiếc bạn không đủ điều kiện" | "Đây là điều tôi nghĩ phù hợp hơn với bạn" |
| Redirect to homepage | Provide a genuinely valuable alternative |
| No next step | Specific milestone to come back to |
| Tone: apologetic or cold | Tone: warm, direct, genuinely helpful |
| Focus: protecting own ego | Focus: the best outcome for the reader |

**Tone test:** Read the copy back and ask: "If I were the person who just received this, how would I feel?" If the answer is "respected and with a clear direction" — correct tone. If the answer is "rejected and confused" — rewrite.

---

## Copy Length

**SHORT.** The reader of this page is not a buyer right now — do not try to convert them with long copy.

**Target:**
- Desktop: 1 comfortable scroll
- Mobile: Maximum 2 scrolls
- No paragraph longer than 3 sentences

**Priority order (if cutting for mobile):**
1. Headline
2. Explanation paragraph
3. Alternative offer + CTA
4. Keep the door open (Zalo contact)

---

## 5 Common Mistakes on Not-a-Fit Pages

**1. Apologetic tone reduces the perceived value of your offer.**
"Rất tiếc" and "Xin lỗi" sound polite but undermine your authority. If your offer is good, you do not need to apologize for selecting the right people. Use advisor tone, not defensive tone.

**2. Vague explanation makes the reader feel arbitrarily rejected.**
"Lúc này chưa phù hợp" without explaining WHY is meaningless. The reader will fill in the blank with the worst reason they can imagine. Specific explanation — even when difficult to say — is always better than vague reassurance.

**3. Generic or clearly inferior alternative adds insult to injury.**
If you redirect them to something you do not genuinely believe will help, the reader will feel it. The alternative must be genuine, relevant to their specific situation, and presented as a real path — not the leftover parts of your sales funnel.

**4. No specific milestone to return to — you lose the long-term lead.**
"Contact us when ready" is meaningless because the reader does not know what "ready" means. A specific milestone ("when you have 10 paying customers") gives them a clear destination — and every time they move closer to it, they think of you.

**5. Page is too long trying to convert someone not yet ready.**
A not-a-fit page is not a second sales page. The reader has just been told they are not the right fit — if you continue pitching them, you undermine the entire credibility of your exclusion. SHORT = respectful. LONG = desperate.
