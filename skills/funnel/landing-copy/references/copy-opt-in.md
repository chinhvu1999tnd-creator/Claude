# Copy Specification: Opt-in / Lead Magnet / HVCO Page

Reference file for the `landing-copy` skill. Use when producing a Copy Brief for an opt-in page — any page that exchanges a free resource (checklist, guide, template, video) for an email or Zalo contact.

---

## Purpose of This Page

The reader's single thought after this page: **"I want this right now."**

Not "seems interesting." Not "let me think about it." Want it now.

An opt-in page does not sell. It exchanges: you give something genuinely valuable, they give you a contact. Write copy like you're selling and you lose both.

**Awareness stage:** Problem Aware → Solution Aware. The reader knows they have a problem and is looking for a way to solve it, but does not yet know you.

---

## Master Stack

| Framework | Role on the opt-in page |
|-----------|-------------------------|
| **Suby HVCO** | Overall structure — value-first before CTA |
| **Halbert I=B+C** | Each bullet = benefit + curiosity, never feature alone |
| **Deiss Before/After Grid** | Headline and sub-headline communicate transformation |
| **Brunson Hook** | First line must hook — interrupt the scroll pattern |

---

## Output Format: Copy Brief

This page should output a **Copy Brief with 4 sections** in this order:

---

### Section 1: Hero

**Type:** `hero-claim`
**Intent:** Stop the scroll. Communicate the transformation promise and the name of the lead magnet clearly enough that the reader immediately thinks "I want that."
**Typical EMPHASIS keywords:** The transformation outcome, the specific group it's for, the key differentiator (time saved, obstacle removed)
**Visual:** none (opt-in pages should have no distraction — lead magnet mockup image is optional, place as `image-placeholder` if included)

**Copy requirements:**
- Headline: maximum 12 words (Vietnamese) — if more is needed, move extra to sub-headline
- Must include at least one of: curiosity trigger OR clear transformation promise
- Never use the document name as the headline ("Download the Marketing Checklist" — wrong)
- Address the reader directly (you/your), do not describe the product
- Sub-headline: clarify WHO this is for + WHAT mechanism + qualify (removes wrong-fit people, builds trust with right-fit people)
- Sub-headline template: "For [specific avatar] who want [outcome] through [mechanism/approach] — without [common barrier]"

**Three headline formulas to choose from:**

Formula A — Transformation Promise (Deiss Before/After):
`"Cách [Specific Group] [Achieve Result] mà không cần [Pain/Obstacle]"`

Formula B — Curiosity + Number (Halbert Specificity):
`"[Number] [Secrets/Strategies/Reasons] tại sao [Phenomenon they see but don't understand]"`

Formula C — Direct Benefit + Time Frame (Brunson Hook):
`"Nhận [Specific Result] Trong [Time Frame] — Miễn Phí"`

**Do not use:**
- "Tải về miễn phí ngay" — no reason to care
- Jargon the reader doesn't use ("lead magnet framework", "conversion optimization")
- Headlines so broad they speak to no one ("Learn Marketing")
- Questions where the answer is "no": "Bạn có muốn kiếm thêm tiền không?"

**Example Copy Brief output for this section:**
```
**Type:** hero-claim
**Intent:** Reader immediately identifies this as for them and wants the lead magnet right now.
**Emphasis:** tăng đơn, không cần tăng ngân sách, chủ shop online
**Visual:** none

**Copy:**
Cách Chủ Shop Online Tăng Đơn 40% mà không cần Tăng Ngân Sách Quảng Cáo

Dành cho chủ shop bán 10–100 đơn/ngày muốn tăng tỷ lệ quay lại mua của khách hàng cũ
thông qua hệ thống Zalo automation — không cần thuê thêm nhân sự hay biết code.
```

---

### Section 2: Benefits List

**Type:** `benefits-list`
**Intent:** Build desire for the lead magnet by revealing what's inside — each bullet should make the reader feel "I need to know that."
**Typical EMPHASIS keywords:** Specific outcomes, numbers, time savings, contrarian or surprising statements
**Visual:** none

**Copy requirements:**
- 5–6 fascination bullets using Halbert's I=B+C formula: each bullet = Benefit (what they get) + Curiosity (why they need to read to find out). No bullet is feature-only.
- Order: Bullet 1 = strongest promise, most specific. Bullets 2–4 = support and variety (mix emotional + logical). Bullet 5 = second strongest — cliffhanger that makes opt-in feel necessary.
- Format per bullet: `[Symbol] [Specific benefit] — [Curiosity hook / "even if" / surprising twist]`
- Use parenthetical location hints: "(page 4)", "(last section)" — creates tangibility
- Maximum 2 lines per bullet
- Never start a bullet with "Học cách..." (weak and vague)
- "Ngay cả khi..." ("even if...") is one of the strongest I=B+C patterns

**Example Copy Brief output for this section:**
```
**Type:** benefits-list
**Intent:** Reader feels "I can't not have this" after reading each bullet.
**Emphasis:** 90% người dùng AI không biết, lỗi số 1, hệ thống lên lịch, 0 đồng
**Visual:** none

**Copy:**
→ Cách tạo 7 bài content trong 20 phút với AI — và tại sao bài viết bằng AI này lại engage tốt hơn bài viết tay (trang 4)

→ 3 prompt "thần thánh" mà 90% người dùng AI không biết — chúng biến ChatGPT từ chatbot thành trợ lý marketing thật sự

→ Lỗi số 1 khiến content AI của bạn bị khách hàng nhận ra ngay lập tức — và cách fix trong 30 giây

→ Hệ thống lên lịch content 1 tháng trong 1 buổi sáng — bao gồm cả caption, hashtag và giờ đăng tối ưu

→ Bí mật mà các agency triệu đô dùng để scale content mà không tăng nhân sự — và bạn có thể áp dụng ngay hôm nay với 0 đồng chi phí thêm (phần cuối tài liệu — đây là phần quan trọng nhất)
```

---

### Section 3: Social Proof

**Type:** `social-proof`
**Intent:** One line of credibility that reassures the reader they are not the first — others have done this and it worked.
**Typical EMPHASIS keywords:** Specific number, specific result, specific group
**Visual:** none

**Copy requirements:**
- A single line — this is NOT a testimonial block. It is a micro-credibility element.
- Place immediately above or below the CTA button — do not separate it from the action moment.
- Use a non-round number (Ogilvy specificity: "2.847" is more believable than "3000")
- Three format options:
  - Option A: `"[Specific number] người đã tải và đang áp dụng — [short result]"`
  - Option B: `"Đã được dùng bởi [specific group the avatar admires]"`
  - Option C: `"[Name], [role/location]: '[1-sentence quote]'"`
- If no real number yet: use Option B with a real group

**Example Copy Brief output for this section:**
```
**Type:** social-proof
**Intent:** Reader feels reassured — other people like them have already done this.
**Emphasis:** 1.243 người, dùng trong tuần đầu tiên
**Visual:** none

**Copy:**
Đã được 1.243 người làm social media tải và dùng trong tuần đầu tiên.
```

---

### Section 4: CTA Strip

**Type:** `cta-strip`
**Intent:** Reader clicks without friction. The button text confirms what they want, and the micro-copy removes the last remaining hesitation.
**Typical EMPHASIS keywords:** The specific thing they'll receive, free
**Visual:** none

**Copy requirements:**

**CTA button — "I want to..." framing:** Button text speaks from the READER's perspective, not yours. Not "Gửi cho tôi" but "Tôi muốn nhận [what they want]."

Five button formula options:
| Formula | Button text | Best for |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Specific outcome | "Gửi cho tôi [tên tài liệu] ngay" | When the document name is clear and compelling |
| Result-focused | "Tôi muốn tăng đơn hàng — gửi ngay" | When outcome is a direct benefit |
| Access framing | "Mở khóa [X] miễn phí" | When the resource has many components |
| Action + time | "Gửi tài liệu — tôi đọc ngay hôm nay" | When natural urgency is wanted |
| Simple clarity | "Nhận miễn phí ngay" | When the headline is already strong enough |

Do NOT use: "Submit", "Đăng ký", "Gửi", "Nhấn vào đây", "Tìm hiểu thêm"

**Under-button micro-copy:** Remove the last friction point. The reader is about to click but one part of their brain asks "Will they spam me?" Answer it in one line, smaller than the button, lighter than body text.

Three micro-copy templates:
- Privacy: `"🔒 Tôi ghét spam. Email/Zalo của bạn an toàn 100% và bạn có thể hủy bất cứ lúc nào."`
- Friction removal: `"Không cần thẻ tín dụng. Không cần cài ứng dụng. Chỉ cần email là xong."`
- Social + reassurance: `"Cùng với 2.800+ người đã nhận tài liệu này — không ai bị làm phiền cả."`

**Designer note:** Button must be high-contrast against background. Minimum 18px text. Padding sufficient to be tappable on mobile. Place micro-copy immediately below the button — not separated by whitespace.

**Example Copy Brief output for this section:**
```
**Type:** cta-strip
**Intent:** Reader clicks with confidence and no remaining hesitation about spam or commitment.
**Emphasis:** 3 prompt, miễn phí
**Visual:** none

**Copy:**
[Button] Gửi cho tôi 3 prompt này — miễn phí

Không spam. Bạn có thể hủy bất cứ lúc nào.
```

## Page-Level Rules

**Length:** SHORT. Everything above the fold on desktop. On mobile: maximum 2.5 scrolls — headline + sub-headline + 3 bullets + CTA visible in the first 1.5 scrolls.

**Mobile priority order:**
1. Headline (above fold)
2. Sub-headline
3. 3 strongest bullets (hide the other 2 if needed)
4. CTA button (always visible before scrolling all the way down)
5. Social proof + under-CTA micro-copy

**No navigation menu** on this page — distraction killer.
**No external links** off the page.

---

## Tone Guide (Suby Edu-Marketing Frame)

You are a teacher sharing genuine insight, not a salesperson pitching.

Right tone:
- Helpful teacher sharing real insight
- Peer-to-peer: "I found this and want to share" — not "Buy now before it's gone"
- Direct but warm — not formal, not over-casual
- Use "bạn" throughout, not "quý khách"

Wrong tone:
- Fake urgency: "Cơ hội cuối cùng!", "Chỉ còn hôm nay!" — wrong for a free opt-in
- Vague overpromise: "Thay đổi cuộc đời bạn mãi mãi" — not believable
- Constant humble-bragging — opt-in page is not the place to flex
- Over-formal: "Kính gửi quý khách hàng tiềm năng"

## Checklist Before Finalizing Opt-in Copy

**Headline:**
- [ ] Maximum 12 words
- [ ] Has a transformation promise or curiosity trigger
- [ ] Addresses "bạn", does not describe the product
- [ ] Reads naturally out loud — no "marketing-speak"

**Bullets:**
- [ ] Every bullet has benefit + curiosity (I=B+C) — not feature-only
- [ ] Bullet 1 is the strongest
- [ ] Last bullet is a cliffhanger that makes opt-in feel necessary
- [ ] No bullet starts with "Học cách..."

**CTA:**
- [ ] Button text uses "I want to..." framing (reader's perspective)
- [ ] No "Submit", "Đăng ký", "Gửi" standalone
- [ ] Under-CTA micro-copy removes at least one friction point

**Overall:**
- [ ] Entire page visible above fold on desktop
- [ ] No navigation menu
- [ ] No external links off the page
- [ ] Mobile test: CTA button visible within the first 1.5 scrolls
- [ ] No sentence starts with "Chúng tôi" or "Công ty chúng tôi"
