# Style Presets — Quick Reference

## Template Categories

### Dark / Dramatic
- `signal` — deep navy, muted gold, institutional. Best: pitch, webinar
- `pink-script` — black canvas, hot pink, luxury editorial. Best: premium product, coaching
- `studio` — black canvas, electric yellow, design-forward. Best: creative agency, portfolio
- `vellum` — deep navy scholarly, warm yellow serif. Best: academic, executive pitch
- `8-bit-orbit` — neon pixel art arcade. Best: tech product, gaming, edgy brand

### Editorial / Literary
- `grove` — forest green, Playfair, rust accent. Best: coaching, wellness, premium content
- `soft-editorial` — sage/blush/lemon, Cormorant. Best: lifestyle, beauty, mindfulness
- `editorial-forest` — forest green/dusty pink. Best: sustainability, nature brand
- `emerald-editorial` — Bodoni, emerald/navy. Best: luxury, fashion, high-ticket offer
- `biennale-yellow` — Dutch poster, sun-glow. Best: bold brand, event announcement

- `bold-poster` — Shrikhand, fire-engine red. Best: FB/Zalo carousel, announcement
- `peoples-platform` — activist poster, Alfa Slab. Best: community, social cause, launch
