# Expert Frameworks Synthesis

## Russell Brunson — Hook → Story → Offer

**Core law:** "If there's an issue in your funnel, it's always related to either the hook, the story, or the offer."

**The three elements:**
- **Hook** — 5–8 giây quyết định. Headline hoặc video opening dừng scroll.
- **Story** — Build rapport + credibility + desire trước khi pitch. Không có story = cold pitch.
- **Offer** — Không phải product, mà là transformation + everything included + guarantee + price.

**Unique Mechanism principle:** Đặt tên riêng cho method/system của bạn. Tên riêng = demand riêng. Không còn so sánh với đối thủ vì "chỉ có bạn dạy Rainmaker Method."

**Stack method:**
List everything → assign value → show total → reveal actual price. Perceived value >> actual price = obvious yes.

**VSL structure:** Hook (video) → Problem → Solution → Proof → Offer → Guarantee → CTA

## Ryan Deiss — Before → After → Bridge

**Core insight:** "Your product is a vehicle that transports people from a less desirable before state to a more desirable after state."

**Before/After Grid:** Define each state across multiple dimensions:
```
                BEFORE          AFTER
Have:           [pain they have]  [what they'll gain]
Feel:           [emotion now]     [emotion after]
Average day:    [current reality] [future reality]
Status:         [current status]  [desired status]
```

**Application:** Paint "After" state vividly và specifically trước khi reveal offer. Prospect phải imagine themselves in the "After" — thì offer trở thành "the bridge" rõ ràng.

**Customer Value Journey:** Awareness → Engage → Subscribe → Convert → Excite → Ascend → Advocate → Promote. Sales page mainly handles Convert stage — nhưng phải understand where prospect came from.

## Frank Kern — Results in Advance + Story Dominance

**Core philosophy:** By the time prospect reaches the sales page, they should already WANT to buy. Preselling through content, email, ads trước = page chỉ là confirmation.

**"Results in Advance" technique:**
1. Give a genuine win/insight in content (ad, email, video) trước khi pitch
2. Prospect experiences result → automatically attributes it to you
3. Sales page becomes "how to get more of this"

**Story-based selling:** "Entire marketing environment is story-driven." Facts tell, stories sell. Kern's pages often minimal text — heavy story in video.

**Inversion technique:** Open bằng the thing audience LEAST expects bạn nói. Builds curiosity và credibility simultaneously.

## Todd Brown — E5 Method + Belief Sequence

**Core law:** "Deliver the marketing message in the PROPER SEQUENCE so it creates desire by establishing all beliefs the prospect needs to have to buy with confidence."

**Required beliefs hierarchy:**
1. "This problem is real and affects me"
2. "The common solutions don't work / I've tried them"
3. "There IS a better way (the mechanism)"
4. "This specific product delivers the mechanism"
5. "I can do this / It will work for me"
6. "The investment is worth it"
7. "Now is the right time"

**Rule:** Miss any belief → prospect won't buy. Wrong order → page won't convert. The sequence IS the strategy.

**Big Marketing Idea:** One central concept that shifts prospect's entire worldview. When they accept the idea → they need the offer. Not a feature, not a benefit — a perspective shift.
Example: "Your ads are failing not because of budget — but because you're targeting the wrong awareness level."

## Sabri Suby — Dream Outcome + 17-Step System

**Dream Outcome Value Equation (từ Alex Hormozi, Suby adapts):**
```
Value = (Dream Outcome × Likelihood of Achievement)
        ÷ (Time Delay × Effort/Sacrifice)
```
**To increase conversions:**
- ↑ Dream outcome (make it bigger/clearer)
- ↑ Perceived likelihood (more proof, better guarantee)
- ↓ Time delay (faster results, quick wins)
- ↓ Effort/sacrifice (done-for-you, simple system)

**Audience Call-Out:** "For [specific avatar] who [specific situation/desire]" — opens every page.

**Bullet point formula:** Mix 4 types:
- Pain bullets: remind them of the problem
- Benefit bullets: show what they gain
- Secret bullets: "You'll discover [intriguing thing]"
- Fear bullets: what happens if they don't act

**Credibility principle:** "Specificity = credibility. Vagueness = suspicion."

## Eugene Schwartz — 5 Awareness Levels

**The five stages:**
1. **Unaware:** No problem recognition. Lead with shocking fact, story, or bold claim about the world.
2. **Problem Aware:** Knows the pain, not the solutions. Lead with the problem itself.
3. **Solution Aware:** Knows solutions exist, not yours. Lead with mechanism/new approach.
4. **Product Aware:** Knows your offer, hasn't committed. Lead with proof, deal, urgency.
5. **Most Aware:** Ready to buy. Lead with offer, price, CTA.

**Page length corollary:**
- More aware = shorter page needed
- Less aware = longer page needed (more education, more trust-building)

**Critical warning:** "Do not educate an already educated market. They know they need the solution — just show them yours is the best one."

## Claude Hopkins — Scientific Advertising

**Specificity principle:** "Actual figures are not generally discounted. Specific facts, when stated, have their full weight and effect."
- "47 clients" > "many clients"
- "Tăng 34% trong 6 tuần" > "tăng đáng kể"
- "Tiết kiệm 3.2 giờ/ngày" > "tiết kiệm nhiều thời gian"

**Reason-why copy:** Always give a REASON for every claim. "Because..." is the most powerful word in copywriting.

**Test everything:** Headlines, CTAs, prices, guarantees. "Advertising is a science, not art."

---

## Gary Halbert — The Great Copywriter

**Market first, copy second:** "The most important thing is not the copy — it's the market. A starving crowd beats brilliant copy."

**Write like you talk:** "Clear, personal, human. Read your copy aloud. If it sounds like a brochure, rewrite it."

**The Boron Letters principle:** Study successful sales letters by copying them by hand. Absorb the rhythm and structure of what works.

**Foundation rule:** Understand your prospect better than they understand themselves. Know their fears, desires, frustrations, dreams — then speak directly to those.

---

## David Ogilvy — Positioning + Headlines

**Headlines are 80% of the job:** "If you haven't done something important with your headline, you've wasted 80% of the client's money."

**Long copy works:** "The more you tell, the more you sell. Research has proved this." (Contested but true for cold traffic.)

**Positioning principle:** How you position the offer matters more than the offer itself. Position as the leader, the specialist, the unique approach.

## Synthesis: 10 Universal Laws

Across all experts, these principles appear in every framework:

1. **Sequence matters more than content** — Right message, wrong order = no conversion (Brown + Brunson)
2. **Story before pitch** — Always establish rapport through narrative first (Kern + Halbert)
3. **Specificity = credibility** — Numbers, names, dates, specific outcomes (Hopkins + Suby)
4. **Awareness determines approach** — Know where your prospect is starting from (Schwartz)
5. **Belief establishment before offer** — All required beliefs must be in place (Brown)
6. **Before/After framing** — Transform abstract benefit into lived experience (Deiss)
7. **Unique mechanism creates demand** — Name your method, own the category (Brunson)
8. **Risk reversal mandatory** — Guarantee removes last barrier (Halbert + Brunson)
9. **Urgency must be real** — Fake scarcity destroys trust permanently (all experts)
10. **CTA must be crystal clear** — Ambiguous CTA = no action (Ogilvy + Hopkins)
